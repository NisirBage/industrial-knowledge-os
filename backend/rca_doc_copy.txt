Root Cause Analysis Report — Pump P-101

Pump P-101 failed due to Valve V-2201A blockage during the March 2024
incident. The bearing wear on P-101 caused excessive vibration, which was
detected during the January 2025 inspection.

Note: the January 2025 inspection log states Valve V-2201A was closed at
the time of inspection, while the March 2024 maintenance record states
Valve V-2201A was open during the incident.
