Failure Report — Pump P-101 Drive-End Bearing

Report Date: 2024-03-22
Failure Mode: Bearing wear / suction starvation
Equipment: Pump P-101 (Sundyne LMV-311)
Related asset: Valve V-2201A

Description:

Drive-end bearing on P-101 failed to the point of requiring unscheduled
replacement on 2024-03-22, approximately 3 weeks earlier than the
scheduled April replacement window flagged in the March 10 inspection
report. Failure mode is consistent with suction starvation caused by Valve
V-2201A being in the closed position at the time of the high-vibration trip,
per Section 9 of the OEM manual's documented failure modes.

Downtime: 4 hours (unplanned).
Personnel involved: 2 maintenance technicians, 1 reliability engineer.
Parts replaced: drive-end bearing assembly (part BRG-4471-DE).
Estimated cost impact: parts + labor + downtime, approximately $8,200.

Preventive action assigned: implement shared valve-state logging across
crews (see SOP-RE-014 revision) to prevent a recurrence of an
undocumented valve state change causing suction starvation on a downstream
asset.
