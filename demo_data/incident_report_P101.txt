Root Cause Analysis Report — Pump P-101 Failure

Incident Date: 2024-03-22
Report Author: T. Nakamura, Reliability Engineering
Incident Classification: Unplanned shutdown, Severity: Medium

Summary:

Pump P-101 tripped on high vibration alarm at 14:32 on 2024-03-22. Root cause
investigation determined that Valve V-2201A, the upstream isolation valve,
was found in the CLOSED position at the time of the trip — contrary to the
expected normal-operation OPEN state — causing a partial flow restriction
that starved P-101's suction and accelerated bearing wear beyond the level
already flagged in the March 2024 maintenance log and the March 10 inspection
report.

Pump P-101 failed due to Valve V-2201A blockage during the March 2024
incident. The bearing wear on P-101 caused excessive vibration, which was
detected during the routine vibration monitoring, escalating to the trip.

Corrective actions taken:
1. Valve V-2201A reopened and confirmed OPEN; flow restored.
2. P-101 drive-end bearing replaced (preempting the scheduled April
   replacement window).
3. Root cause: valve position was changed during an adjacent line's
   maintenance work without updating P-101's operating log — a
   communication gap between crews, not an equipment defect.

Follow-up action: implement a shared valve-state log across maintenance
crews working on interconnected lines.
