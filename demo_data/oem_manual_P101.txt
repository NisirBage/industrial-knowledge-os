OEM Manual Excerpt — Sundyne Centrifugal Pump, Model LMV-311
Applies to: Pump P-101

Section 4: Bearing Maintenance

Drive-end and non-drive-end bearings should be inspected at intervals not
exceeding 2,000 operating hours or 6 months, whichever comes first. Vibration
alert threshold: 3.5 mm/s RMS (ISO 10816 Zone C boundary for this pump class).
Vibration trip threshold: 7.0 mm/s RMS.

Section 7: Upstream Isolation Valve Requirements

This pump model requires a full-bore isolation valve (such as Valve V-2201A
in this installation) upstream of the suction flange. The valve MUST remain
fully open during normal operation. Operating the pump with the upstream
valve partially closed or closed risks suction starvation, cavitation, and
accelerated bearing wear — consistent with the failure mode described in
Section 9.

Section 9: Common Failure Modes

- Suction starvation (upstream valve closed/restricted): symptoms include
  rising vibration, bearing wear, and eventual high-vibration trip.
- Seal degradation from age or chemical exposure.
- Misalignment following reinstallation after maintenance.
