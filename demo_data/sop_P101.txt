Standard Operating Procedure — Pump P-101 Startup and Isolation

Document ID: SOP-RE-014
Standard Reference: API 610, OSHA Lockout/Tagout (29 CFR 1910.147)

1. Pre-Startup Checklist

   1.1 Confirm Valve V-2201A is fully OPEN before requesting permission to
       start P-101. Do not start the pump against a closed or partially
       closed upstream valve.
   1.2 Confirm bearing housing lubrication level per the OEM manual.
   1.3 Confirm no active lockout/tagout tags on P-101 or V-2201A.

2. Isolation for Maintenance

   2.1 Obtain a maintenance permit before closing Valve V-2201A.
   2.2 Apply lockout/tagout to both P-101 and V-2201A.
   2.3 Log the valve state change in the shared equipment log, including
       crew name, time, and reason.
   2.4 Any crew working on adjacent lines that share V-2201A must confirm
       with Rotating Equipment Maintenance before changing its state.

3. Post-Maintenance Restart

   3.1 Confirm V-2201A has been reopened and the change logged.
   3.2 Confirm vibration baseline before returning P-101 to full duty.

Personnel required: one licensed maintenance technician, one permit
authority for isolation work.
