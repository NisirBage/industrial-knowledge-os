Inspection Report — Pump P-101 / Valve V-2201A

Inspection Date: 2024-03-10
Inspector: M. Okonkwo (Certified Mechanical Inspector)
Standard Reference: API 610 (Centrifugal Pumps for Petroleum Industry)

Scope: Quarterly mechanical integrity inspection of Pump P-101 and its
upstream isolation valve V-2201A.

Findings:

1. Pump P-101 drive-end bearing shows measurable wear consistent with the
   March 2024 maintenance log entry. Vibration signature confirms elevated
   amplitude at the bearing pass frequency. Recommend bearing replacement
   within 30 days.

2. Valve V-2201A was found in the OPEN position at the time of inspection,
   consistent with normal operating configuration. No leakage observed at the
   valve seat.

3. No corrosion or external damage observed on either asset.

Compliance status: P-101 and V-2201A both comply with API 610 inspection
interval requirements. Next inspection due: 2024-06-10.

Inspector recommendation: Schedule bearing replacement for P-101 before the
next quarterly inspection to avoid unplanned downtime.
